Our story

Your project isn’t finished… until SpartaCraft finishes it.

Since 1967, SpartaCraft has built its reputation on craftsmanship, quality and dependable American manufacturing.

As a federally recognized manufacturer, we specialize in premium custom woodworking products that serve customers across the United States.

Located in Connelly Springs, North Carolina, just outside Hickory, our 72,000-square-foot manufacturing and distribution facility combines decades of woodworking expertise with modern production capabilities.

We source American materials whenever possible and keep investing in the processes that deliver consistent results. Our team works closely with every customer to meet their exact specifications.

Four markets. One mission.

01

Military Flag Cases & Urns

Premium military flag cases and memorial urns, built from American-sourced hardwoods and finished with the respect these products deserve.

02

Custom Furniture Manufacturing & Finishing

From individual components to complete collections, for furniture manufacturers and designers who demand quality and consistency.

03

Hardwood Flooring Finishing

Finishing services for regional hardwood flooring manufacturers — durable finishes that bring out the natural grain.

04

Wood & Finish Chip Samples

Precision sample chips that help manufacturers, distributors, retailers and designers show their finishes with confidence.

To provide exceptional craftsmanship, dependable service, and products that exceed expectations.

Questions

How do I become a SpartaCraft dealer?

Fill out the dealer application and one of our customer representatives will get right back to you.

I’m already a dealer. How do I create my online login?

Go to Dealer login and choose “Create your log in here”. You’ll need your SpartaCraft account number (it’s on your invoice) and your billing ZIP code. If you can’t find it, email sales@spartacraft.com.

I forgot my username or password.

On the Dealer login page, select “Forgot Username” or “Forgot Password”. We’ll email the address on file.

What can I do in the dealer portal?

Place orders, view your price list and availability, save favorites, track shipments and backorders, review order history, update account details and pay open invoices securely.

Can families order directly?

Yes. Personalize a case in our shop and send an order request — our team confirms engraving, pricing and shipping before anything is built. You can also call (828) 397-4630.
